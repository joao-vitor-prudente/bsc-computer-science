# Quantifiers

- $\exists x[P(x)] \equiv P(x_1) \lor P(x_2) \lor ... \lor P(x_n)$
- $\forall x[P(x)] \equiv P(x_1) \land P(x_2) \land ... \land P(x_n)$

## Applying De Morgan's Law

- $\lnot \exists x[P(x)] \equiv \lnot P(x_1) \land \lnot P(x_2) \land ... \land \lnot P(x_n) \equiv \forall x[\lnot P(x)]$
- $\lnot \forall x[P(x)] \equiv \lnot P(x_1) \lor \lnot P(x_2) \lor ... \lor \lnot P(x_n) \equiv \exists x[\lnot P(x)]$
